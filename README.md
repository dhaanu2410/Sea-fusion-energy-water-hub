# 🌊 Sea Fusion Energy & Water Hub

**Exploring the Integration of Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion and Low-Temperature Thermal Desalination**

## 📌 Project Overview

Sea Fusion Energy & Water Hub is a conceptual energy-and-water solution developed for the **Ocean Hackathon 2026, Chennai Edition**, under challenge **CHE02: Sea Fusion Energy & Water Hub**.

The project explores the potential integration of **Open-Cycle Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OC-OTEC)** and **Low-Temperature Thermal Desalination (LTTD)** to address freshwater and energy challenges in island communities.

**Case Study:** Agatti Island, Lakshadweep, India

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Island communities face challenges such as limited freshwater availability, dependence on external resources, and the cost of supplying reliable electricity. Desalination can help address freshwater scarcity, but its energy requirements and environmental impacts must also be considered.

This project explores an ocean-based approach that combines freshwater production with potential electricity generation.

## 💡 Proposed Concept

### 1. Open-Cycle Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OC-OTEC)

OC-OTEC uses warm surface seawater under reduced pressure to produce water vapour. The vapour can expand through a suitable low-pressure turbine connected to an electrical generator.

### 2. Low-Temperature Thermal Desalination (LTTD)

LTTD uses the temperature difference between warm surface seawater and cold deep seawater. Under low-pressure conditions, a portion of the warm seawater evaporates. The vapour is condensed using cold seawater to produce freshwater.

The collected water requires appropriate quality testing and post-treatment before distribution.

### 3. Integrated Energy and Water System

The proposed concept investigates the integration of OC-OTEC and LTTD, including the potential use of generated electricity for auxiliary equipment such as pumps and vacuum systems.

Shared infrastructure and improved efficiency are possibilities to evaluate, not yet demonstrated outcomes.

## 🌱 Expected Benefits

- Explore an alternative approach to freshwater supply for island communities.
- Investigate the relationship between ocean thermal energy and desalination.
- Evaluate potential coordination of energy generation and water production.
- Consider seawater intake, discharge management, and freshwater quality requirements.

These are potential benefits that depend on further technical and environmental validation.

## 📚 Research Areas

The project draws on research into:

- Open-Cycle Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OC-OTEC).
- Low-Temperature Thermal Desalination (LTTD).
- Ocean temperature profiles and seasonal variation.
- Energy requirements for pumping and vacuum systems.
- Freshwater quality and seawater discharge management.
