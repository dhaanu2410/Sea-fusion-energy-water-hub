Analogy: The Intelligent Hotel Manager

Imagine a hotel on an island with four kitchens producing food, a cold-storage room, and guests demanding meals at varying times:

  • Solar: Kitchen producing high output during sunny hours.
  • Wind: Kitchen whose output fluctuates with wind conditions.
  • Wave / OTEC: Kitchens providing steady ocean baseload and changing wave power.
  • Battery: The cold-storage room.
With an intelligent manager, the system predicts demand, checks supplies, stores excess, and coordinates resources in real time.

Dual-Intelligence Architecture

A. Deep Learning (The Predictor)

Acts like a weather forecaster, learning historical patterns to predict solar, wind, wave, and consumer demand for the next 24 hours.

B. Metaheuristic Optimization (The Decision-Maker)

Algorithms such as Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO) evaluate operating schedules for low cost while respecting limits.
